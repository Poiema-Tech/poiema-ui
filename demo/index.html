Write semantic HTML, add pm-* classes, and let the tokens do the rest. The same markup works in Next.js, SvelteKit, Vue, Astro or a static page.

The message that must be clear

This isn't a component library. It's a way of speaking. The classes are just the vocabulary.

What it is for

Every product we ship should feel like it was made by the same hands: calm, honest and easy to follow.

This is not a template.

Each team keeps the freedom to compose its screens. These are the base decisions (color, type, shape, motion and voice) that keep everything recognizably ours.

03

motion rises, floats and settles.

  • Sections rise 28px and settle over 900ms on a soft curve.
  • Objects float on a 7s breath and lean toward your pointer.
  • Materials cross-fade, like the gem in the hero.
  • Nothing bounces. Reduced motion turns it all off.
The message that must be clear

Motion gives weight, not entertainment. If you notice it, it's too much.

06

how we talk.

the message is bigger than the messenger.

  • We speak as we, to our family. We are never selling.
  • We say what something is not, then what it is.
  • Three short beats, each with a period: plan. focus. finish.
  • Buttons start with a verb. Feedback ends with a ✓.
The message that must be clear

Every section closes with the one thing a reader must take away.

Not more words. Clearer ones.

Important

Freedom in how you say it should never change what it means.

What every screen must keep

  • The gray room and the ink actions.
  • Lowercase headlines that end with a period.
  • One clear message per section.
  • Soft shapes: 40px cards, pills, rings.

What is up to each team

  • The layout and order of sections.
  • Which object tells your story.
  • The language and examples you use.
